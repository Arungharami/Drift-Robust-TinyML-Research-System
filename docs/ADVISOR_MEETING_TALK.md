The core concern is chronological drift: sensor behavior changes across batches, so a random train/test split can blend time periods and give an optimistic estimate. I therefore treat `FIXED_ORIGIN` as the primary prospective protocol, `EXPANDING_WINDOW` as a secondary adaptation analysis, and `IID_DIAGNOSTIC` as diagnostic only.

The project portal is evidence-driven. Its status counts are generated from the pipeline registry, not inferred from a dashboard. Stages 10 and 11 executed the planned fidelity and chronological-stability analyses, but the tested candidate claims were unsupported. I want advice on how to interpret that result: whether it primarily indicates weak explanation behavior, definitions that need refinement, or an additional sensitivity analysis.
